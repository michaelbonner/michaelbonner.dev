# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Friends, local visitors, and other readers of michaelbonner.dev who want a trusted personal recommendation for where to eat in and around Salt Lake City.

## Product Purpose

michaelbonner.dev is Michael Bonner's public personal site. It shares his work, writing, projects, and useful personal recommendations. The restaurant page helps visitors quickly find a place that fits their location, budget, and preferred food.

## Brand Personality

Personal, practical, and opinionated. The writing should sound like a recommendation from someone who has eaten there, not promotional copy from a travel directory.

## Anti-references

- Generic travel-directory pages packed with sponsored placements and stock photography.
- Corporate restaurant-review apps with dense ratings, badges, and engagement mechanics.
- Polished marketing copy that hides the author's real opinion.

## Design Principles

- Make Michael's point of view visible through concise ratings, notes, and recommendations.
- Help visitors decide quickly without burying the useful details.
- Keep interactions direct and familiar, especially filtering, maps, and directions.
- Use real project and restaurant imagery rather than fabricated or generic visuals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, reduced motion preferences, and interfaces that do not rely on color or pointer hover alone.
